# Onze
## Platform
web
## Confirmed brief
French creative portfolio and t-shirt shop, design x football. Three primary pages Accueil, Portfolio, Shop. Home uses supplied night field image and actual distressed ball. Scroll shot into upper-left corner of right goal. Portfolio project index and detail pages. Shop index and product detail placeholders. Shared navigation and contact footer.
## Open decisions
Actual projects, product photos, stock, prices, checkout, public contact address, logo and definitive copy are not supplied. All demonstration work and products explicitly marked as maquettes. No live sale or invented contact.
## Direction contract
Experience-led homepage with restrained white typography on original dark landscape, one scroll-driven authored moment. Warm off-white editorial index pages, deep ink typography and blue accent. No decorative gradients, purple, glass or generated photoreal artwork. Plain static HTML/CSS/JS. Portfolio and Shop are interactive structural previews, not published real catalogs.
## Motion
Ball leads scroll; image and nav stay static. Copy fades in place to free shot path. Native sticky within two viewport hero; no scroll-jacking. Only transform and opacity. Reduced motion static ball and no scrub. Keyboard interactions instant.
